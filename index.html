Guide · How to Play

  • Tap anywhere to flap and fly.
  • Dodge pipes and traffic — don’t hit!
  • Collect coins 🪙 in gaps for combos; a missed coin lowers the multiplier one step instead of wiping it.
  • Grab gifts 📦 — they stack in Mystery Rewards.
  • Power-ups: Shield, Turbo, Magnet, Slow-mo, Ghost (brief).
  • Modes: Classic, Hard, Time Attack, Daily, Practice & more.
  • Mystery Rewards: every 10 boxes = 1 spin (444–999 coins). History saves past spins.
  • Skill: edge pass = CLOSE; center pass = PERFECT.
  • Feel: Classic is smoother & forgiving; Hard and One Life stay strict.
  • Run grade: S–E on the summary — PERFECT & CLOSE boost it.
  • Daily missions: 3 rotate each day (PERFECT, combo, gifts, fly…).
  • Chase events: DANGER bosses every stretch — survive the timer.
  • Share: preview your score card before sending.
  • Perfect rail: center the cyan/gold rail for PERFECT.
  • Practice: follow the ghost bird through gaps.
  • Power VFX: Slow-mo purple edge, Ghost afterimage, Turbo speed lines.
  • One Life: heart HUD — no continue.
  • Sukoon / Relax: optional 1/3/5-minute slow flight, wider gaps, gentle non-lethal bumps, and no score or rewards.
  • Character looks: Garage has six local-only optional mascot appearances; “Use equipped bird” returns to the existing unlocked bird.
  • Mystery wheel: Spin once is a full 15s decelerate into the segment; Spin all uses 15s / 8s / 5s tiering.
