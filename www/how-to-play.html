How to Play · Kaise Khelein

A quick guide in English and Roman Urdu. Your scores, coins, and unlocks stay in this browser on this device.

English Roman Urdu

English

  • Controls: Tap or click to flap; on a keyboard, press Space. During a run, press Esc or use Pause; tap Play once, then flap to stay airborne. Leaving the tab or app pauses the run; choose Resume when you return.
  • MAGIC 🪄: Get +1 free item each local calendar day. Open MAGIC 🪄 from the menu or tap the gameplay button to use one. It steers toward the next gap and ignores pipes for 10 seconds; autopilot lasts 7 seconds, then control returns for the final 3. Pause freezes its timer. Rewarded ads are unavailable until an ad SDK is connected; future rewards will be capped at 2 per day with a 10-hour cooldown.
  • Modes: Classic is the forgiving endless run. Daily uses today's local-date challenge and best score. Time Attack lasts 60 seconds; Hard is faster; No Coin removes coin pickups; Challenge has stages; One Life has no retry; Practice is for learning.
  • Sukoon / Relax: Choose 1, 3 or 5 minutes of slower, wide-gap flying. Bumps do not end a run; there is no score, reward pressure, or saved record.
  • Character looks: In Garage, choose one of six optional mascot appearances or “Use equipped bird.” The choice is saved only on this device and never changes bird unlocks, passives, coins, or records.
  • Scoring: A normal gap pass is +1. Pass near the gap center for PERFECT (+3). A CLOSE near-miss pass earns at least +5; the active combo multiplier can increase pass points. Avoid pipes, the ground, the ceiling, and traffic.
  • Coins and gifts: Collect coins in gaps; keep collecting to grow the coin combo (up to ×5). A missed coin gently lowers the multiplier one step instead of wiping it. Pick up gift boxes for Mystery Rewards. Coins, gifts, scores, and unlocks are saved locally.
  • Power-ups: Shield can save one hit; Turbo speeds you up; Magnet pulls coins toward you; Slow-mo briefly slows the action; Ghost briefly lets you pass through hazards.
  • Install and play offline: Open the game once while online and wait for it to finish loading. In your browser menu choose Install or Add to Home Screen (wording varies). After the first successful load, the game shell and guide are cached for offline play.
  • Troubleshooting: If it will not start offline, reconnect and open it once so caching can finish, then try again. If the game is still blank, reload after reconnecting. Clearing browser site data removes the offline cache and may erase local progress; private browsing may not retain saves.

Daily challenges reset at local midnight. A retry restarts today's seeded challenge; it is not a recording of an earlier run.

Roman Urdu

  • Controls: Tap ya click karo to flap ho. Keyboard par Space dabao. Run ke darmiyan Esc ya Pause dabao; Play aik dafa, phir hawa mein rehne ke liye tap karte raho. App ya tab chhorne par run pause ho jayega; wapas aa kar Resume dabayen.
  • MAGIC 🪄: Har local din +1 free item milta hai. Menu mein MAGIC 🪄 kholo ya run ke MAGIC button se aik use karo. Yeh aglay gap ki taraf steer karta aur 10 seconds pipes se bachata hai; pehle 7 seconds autopilot, phir aakhri 3 seconds control aap ke paas. Pause par timer rukta hai. Ad SDK connect honay tak rewarded ads dastiyab nahi; mustaqbil ke rewards roz 2 aur 10-hour cooldown tak mehdood honge.
  • Modes: Classic narm endless run hai. Daily aaj ki local-date challenge aur best score rakhta hai. Time Attack 60 seconds ka hai; Hard tez hai; No Coin mein coins nahi; Challenge stages hain; One Life mein retry nahi; Practice seekhne ke liye hai.
  • Sukoon / Relax: 1, 3 ya 5 minute ki aahista, khulay gaps wali flight. Halki takkar run khatam nahi karti; score, inaam ka dabao ya saved record nahi.
  • Character looks: Garage mein chhay mascot looks ya “Use equipped bird” chunein. Pasand isi device par save hoti hai; unlocks, bird bonus, coins ya records nahi badalte.
  • Score: Aam gap pass par +1. Gap ke beech se guzro to PERFECT (+3). CLOSE near-miss pass kam az kam +5 deta hai; combo multiplier points barha sakta hai. Pipes, zameen, ceiling aur traffic se bacho.
  • Coins aur gifts: Gaps mein coins lo; lagataar coins se coin combo barhta hai (×5 tak). Coin miss ho to multiplier sirf aik darja kam hota hai, poora reset nahi hota. Gift boxes uthao aur Mystery Rewards ke liye jama karo. Coins, gifts, scores aur unlocks isi browser mein save hote hain.
  • Power-ups: Shield aik hit se bacha sakta hai; Turbo raftaar barhata hai; Magnet coins apni taraf kheenchta hai; Slow-mo kuch dair game dheema karta hai; Ghost kuch dair hazards se guzarne deta hai.
  • Install aur offline: Pehli dafa internet ke sath game kholo aur loading mukammal hone do. Browser menu mein Install ya Add to Home Screen chuno. Pehli kamyab loading ke baad game aur yeh guide offline cache ho jate hain.
  • Masla ho to: Offline start na ho to internet jor kar game aik dafa kholo taa-ke cache mukammal ho; phir dobara try karo. Blank screen aaye to reconnect karke reload karo. Browser site data clear karne se offline cache aur local progress mit sakti hai; private mode saves nahi rakhta ho sakta.

Daily challenge local waqt ke mutabiq raat 12 baje reset hoti hai. Retry aaj ki seeded challenge dobara shuru karta hai; yeh purane run ki recording nahi.
